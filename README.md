> Our CEO said 'we're a family' 14 times this quarter; families don't usually schedule the layoffs for Friday.

## Why this line

The joke lands on a contradiction everyone in an office recognizes: the warm "we're a family" language companies repeat — here counted, 14 times this quarter — set against the cold timing of layoffs scheduled for Friday. The specific count makes the corporate cliché feel measured and hollow, and the twist in the second half reframes "family" literally, so the punchline arrives in a single, clean reversal within the 200-character limit.
